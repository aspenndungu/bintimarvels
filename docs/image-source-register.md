## Excluded by default

- Identifiable schoolchildren, children’s-home participants and school group galleries unless Binti has confirmed the original project’s continuing website/media-consent basis. The deployed school-outreach archive derivative is deliberately pixelated; the identifiable original is not published.
- Participant-heavy prison photographs.
- Publisher-owned Business Daily/Nation photographs.
- Social contact sheets, research screenshots and generated marketing drafts.
- Images containing bank, Till, Paybill, account or contribution instructions.
- Generated bundle images that show a different pack quantity from the product being sold.
- Generated product renders with altered packaging text, symbols, expiry marks or artwork. A Codex/GPT Image 2 draft was reviewed and rejected for this reason; it was not added to `public/`.
- A product-free Codex/GPT Image 2 studio background was also reviewed but not deployed because combining it with the opaque first-party pack photograph produced an obviously layered composition. The exact first-party product photograph remains the public hero.
