Research date: 15 July 2026

## Interpretation of the brief

The spoken references to “B2” and “Brembo” are treated as Binti and Mrembo. The proposed public page name is **Binti Charity**. Binti remains a Kenyan private company. The page will explain partner-funded product supply and past community work; it will not solicit public donations, publish bank-account details, issue donation receipts or describe Binti as an NGO.

## Legacy-site material that must not be copied as-is

- The Binti Project page directly solicits donations and links to a payment page. That conflicts with the current private-company model.
- The page uses unsupported or stale impact totals, school-absence figures, product-performance claims, FDA wording and pricing comparisons.
- The page contains draft placeholders such as “INSERT GIRLS DIGNITY KIT”.
- A legacy project post is still Lorem Ipsum.
- Historical impact figures range from 300,000 to 600,000+ with no shared definition or reproducible methodology. Do not publish an impact total until the company creates a dated claims register.
- Do not publish any bank, Till, Paybill, account number, donor payment instruction, founder private data or vulnerable-person details on Binti Charity.

## Customer-ready content direction

1. Lead the home page with Mrembo as a locally made Kenyan period-care product from Binti Marvels.
2. Explain the transition plainly: imported Binti Pads built the mission; the 2025 pause forced a better model; locally produced Mrembo carries the work forward.
3. Rename Partnerships to Binti Charity, but keep the conversion route as a commercial quote for funded product supply, not a donation checkout.
4. Use dated project stories and named collaborators instead of unsupported cumulative impact numbers.
5. Add a linked press section and a complete leadership section.
6. Replace internal phrases such as “prototype catalogue”, “price basis”, “production controls”, “recorded order patterns” and “selected Till” with customer-facing copy.
7. Keep Direct M-Pesa “coming soon” short and redirect the current order to WhatsApp without exposing technical implementation details.
