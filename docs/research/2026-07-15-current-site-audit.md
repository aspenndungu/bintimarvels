# Binti/Mrembo rebuild audit before the second design pass

Audit date: 15 July 2026
Branch: `feat/direct-mpesa-site-rebuild`
Evidence: desktop screenshots for 13 routes, iPhone screenshots for 8 principal routes, current asset contact sheet, direct image inspection, legacy WordPress pages, public social/press research and current source review.

## Verdict

The visual system is promising: burgundy, cream, gold, serif display type and rounded cards already feel recognisably premium. The site is not customer-ready because it exposes internal implementation language, under-explains the Binti-to-Mrembo transition, has too little real-world proof, leaves important pages visually thin and offers a delivery model the owner has rejected.

The next pass should preserve the design language while replacing sparse placeholders with evidence-backed story, real imagery and a complete location-to-delivery journey.

## Cross-site defects

1. **Internal notes are public.** “Prototype catalogue”, “preview model”, “recorded order patterns”, “selected Till”, “production controls”, “final Binti approval” and similar language reads like an internal review document, not a shop.
2. **Binti-to-Mrembo is too vague.** A previous Binti customer cannot quickly understand what changed, what stayed, why Mrembo exists or that Mrembo is locally contract-manufactured in Kenya.
3. **The trust layer is missing.** Named collaborations, event sponsors, earned media and current distribution signals are absent even though public evidence exists.
4. **Real imagery is too sparse.** The site relies heavily on one polished hero and repeated pack images while company-owned leadership, event and current Mrembo collaboration imagery is available.
5. **Some current assets should not ship.** Generated bundle artworks visually contradict their paid quantities; the online price graphic promises free delivery; old Binti product imagery must be clearly historical; school images with identifiable minors need rights confirmation.
6. **Several pages end before a persuasive story develops.** The legal pages are expected to be text-led, but Story, Binti Circles, Partnerships/Charity, Contact and Delivery need richer hierarchy and visual anchors.
7. **Mobile pages become very long and narrow without enough visual rhythm.** Product cards and the homepage need stronger section breaks, shorter copy blocks and repeat conversion points.
8. **The floating WhatsApp button competes with form fields and content on small screens.** Safe-area spacing and form-aware placement need attention.
9. **Footer information architecture repeats weak route names.** “Partnerships” must become “Binti Charity”; social channels and press/transition links should be easier to find.
10. **No page should present unverified cumulative impact totals.** Existing public figures conflict and have no common method.

## Route-by-route findings

### Home

Strengths: premium hero, strong palette, visible shop action and useful product entry point.

Defects:
- The hero does not foreground “Made in Kenya” or explain Binti/Mrembo.
- “A Kenyan product vision” is hedged and immediately followed by internal claims-review wording.
- The product section exposes “Prototype catalogue”.
- No current social proof, partner proof, press links, distribution areas or founder voice.
- The community/partnership cards are generic and use the wrong page name.
- Desktop composition feels narrow relative to the viewport; mobile becomes a very long stack with repeated card shapes.

Required upgrade: local-manufacturing trust bar, clear transition section, current product story, selected real-world proof, event/partner strip, press section, founder quote/video and a final shopping CTA.

### Shop

Strengths: prices are clear, basket controls are visible and product hierarchy is consistent.

Defects:
- “Catalogue preview” and the price-evidence paragraph are internal.
- Repeated identical single-pack imagery makes four bundles feel redundant.
- Bundle cards need a clearer “what you receive” hierarchy without pretending that one photographed pack is the full bundle.
- No “why Mrembo” product proof from the pack: 8 regular pads per pack, unscented, cotton-feel soft and comfortable.
- No stockist/WhatsApp alternative or location-aware delivery bridge.

Required upgrade: customer copy, feature row grounded in package text, honest bundle labels, “buy online / find a nearby stockist” choices and delivery-location CTA.

### Checkout

Strengths: server quote, honest payment status and WhatsApp continuation avoid fake checkout.

Defects:
- Empty-basket state occupies an entire page and lacks product recommendations.
- Current delivery zones and warehouse pickup contradict the new brief.
- Internal explanations about order history, Daraja, the selected Till and no-card architecture must disappear.
- No address suggestion, map pin, exact route distance or distance fee.
- No durable delivery details for future Dispatch handoff.

Required upgrade: address search, map selection, exact destination confirmation, server-side Google route distance, configurable distance band, delivery estimate, Google Maps share link and WhatsApp/order handoff. No warehouse collection.

### Our Story

Strengths: attractive split hero and correct instinct to connect Binti to Mrembo.

Defects:
- Too short for a founder/company transition story.
- Founder/leadership section is absent despite public images for Lorna, Beth and Barnabas.
- No dated timeline, “why the pause”, local contract-manufacturing explanation, press links or 2026 interview.
- Current old-product portrait needs a historical caption so it does not confuse Mrembo shoppers.

Required upgrade: 2020–2026 timeline, “what changed / what stayed”, complete leadership grid, locally-made explanation, click-to-play founder interview and press cards.

### Binti Circles

Strengths: warm tone and good visual identity.

Defects:
- Current copy defines a broad future community rather than showing real activity.
- Hero uses marketing artwork rather than current community evidence.
- Cards are generic and do not explain how Circles relates to Binti Charity, Mrembo customers or current events.
- CTA is weak and the page ends quickly.

Required upgrade: evidence-based current programmes/conversations, clear distinction from Binti Charity, recent Menstrual Health Day/community card and a simple interest/contact route without promising unsupported programmes.

### Partnerships → Binti Charity

Strengths: current page correctly avoids a fake public-donation checkout.

Defects:
- Wrong name and route.
- “How a partnership works” reveals process details the owner does not want public.
- One school image with identifiable minors is doing too much trust work and creates a safeguarding concern.
- No project archive, current Mrembo proof, named partner/event proof or charity-golf story.
- “Purchase care. Deliver dignity.” is acceptable but the page reads like an operational quotation explainer.

Required upgrade: `/binti-charity` canonical route; legacy `/partnerships` redirect; strong real hero; dated project cards; current Mrembo collaborations; 2024 Binti Charity Golf story and sponsor list; supporter/partner evidence; external source links; contact CTA framed as “Fund product supply” without public donation collection or bank details.

### Contact

Strengths: simple and understandable form.

Defects:
- Large empty space around a small form on desktop.
- No channel cards, response expectation, physical/fulfilment context, social links or enquiry-specific next steps.
- Current form still references the old Partnerships label.

Required upgrade: order/stockist, Binti Charity, wholesale/retail, media and Binti Circles paths; WhatsApp-first action; current social links and customer-safe Mombasa Road fulfilment wording.

### Delivery

Strengths: copy is structured and currently honest.

Defects:
- Entire model is rejected: no warehouse pickup and no generic three-zone price table.
- Public copy exposes “launch testing”, “recorded online orders”, payment infrastructure and operational caveats.
- No live address search, map, route distance, drop-off option or customer-facing explanation of Binti delivery.

Required upgrade: concise delivery promise, map-powered estimate explainer, service boundary, long-distance/drop-off treatment, failed-route fallback and link to checkout location step.

### Privacy

Defects: starts with “Pre-launch draft” and lacks effective date, location-data purpose, Google mapping processor disclosure, retention principles and security/rights route.

Required upgrade: customer-facing policy with effective date, categories, purposes, recipients, map/location handling, rights and contact. Do not invent hard retention periods.

### Terms

Defects: starts with a draft disclaimer, discusses browser prices as non-authoritative in technical language and describes partnership supply defensively.

Required upgrade: clean order formation, price/delivery confirmation, M-Pesa status, Binti Charity funded supply, acceptable use, product information, liability boundary and contact.

### Returns

Defects: draft disclaimer, no clear customer action checklist and a malformed masked `tel:` target.

Required upgrade: report-damage/incorrect-item flow, hygiene boundary, evidence, review and approved replacement/refund process. Do not invent a fixed return window until approved.

### Messaging & Consent

Mostly sound, but visually thin. Add effective date, concise opt-out route and cross-link to Privacy without making unsupported regulatory claims.

### Data Access & Deletion

Mostly sound, but “full control” and “permanently delete” are too absolute because legal/accounting records may need to remain. Align language and visual style with Privacy.

## Delivery architecture findings

The secured server key was tested without exposing it:

- Google Places Autocomplete (New): working.
- Place Details: working.
- Google Routes `computeRoutes`: working in `TWO_WHEELER` mode.
- Google reverse geocoding: working.

The key can stay server-side. The browser can use a keyless map (Leaflet/OpenStreetMap tiles) while address suggestions, place details, reverse geocoding and route distance come through protected same-origin server endpoints. This avoids shipping the server key to the frontend.

The existing dispatch route cache contains a consistent warehouse origin and real route distances, but no approved rider fee card was found. Therefore the fee engine must be configurable and clearly treated as an estimate until Binti provides the final bands.

Do not publish five dummy rider phone numbers. The website should create a delivery-ready destination record; the existing Dispatch workflow can assign one of five configured riders later. Fake public numbers create customer and privacy risk.

## Definition of customer-ready for this pass

- No internal launch, evidence or architecture commentary in public copy.
- No donation checkout, bank details, fake impact numbers or fake partner endorsements.
- Every principal page has a clear purpose, credible proof and next action.
- Binti legacy and Mrembo present-day product are unambiguous.
- “Made in Kenya” is prominent and qualified as local contract manufacturing where detail matters.
- Address, map and distance quote work on mobile and desktop without exposing the Google key.
- Long-distance delivery remains Binti-coordinated; warehouse collection is absent.
- Real collaborators are dated and tied to the specific event/project supported by evidence.
- All screenshots pass three post-implementation visual/copy/function review rounds.
